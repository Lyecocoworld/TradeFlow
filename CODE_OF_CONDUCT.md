# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming environment, we as contributors and maintainers pledge to making participation in the **TradeFlow** project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Project Stewardship

This project is developed and owned by **Lye**. While community feedback and bug reports are welcome, the final direction of the project, architectural decisions, and feature implementations are at the sole discretion of the owner.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

*   Using welcoming and inclusive language.
*   Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
*   Gracefully accepting constructive criticism.
*   Focusing on what is best for the community and the code quality.
*   Showing empathy towards other community members.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

*   The use of sexualized language or imagery and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
*   Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
*   Public or private harassment.
*   Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or electronic address, without explicit permission.
*   Distributing the source code or binaries of TradeFlow in violation of the LICENSE.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by contacting the project owner (**Lye**). All complaints will be reviewed and investigated and will result in a response that is deemed necessary and appropriate to the circumstances.

The project owner has the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned to this Code of Conduct, or to ban temporarily or permanently any contributor for other behaviors that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.
